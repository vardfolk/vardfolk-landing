What's inside

Everything your circle needs

Built for the reality of caregiving — where plans change, people are scattered, and details matter.

Document scan & extraction
Upload discharge papers, insurance documents, or doctor's notes. Vardfolk reads them and pulls out medications, follow-up appointments, and action items — offering to add them directly to your circle. No re-typing, no missed details.
Shared task board
Assign tasks, set due dates, and track what's done — everyone sees the same list. No more wondering who's handling what.
Medications & reminders
Track medications, doses, and schedules. Get notified when it's time — so nothing gets missed.
Appointment calendar
Log appointments with location, notes, and who's attending. Your circle always knows what's coming up.
Care updates
Post daily updates with mood notes so distant family members stay connected to what's really happening.
Family messaging
One dedicated thread for your circle. No more scattered texts across five different apps.
Document vault
Store insurance cards, discharge summaries, and legal documents — organized, searchable, and always accessible.
Care Sheet
A shareable one-page summary of your loved one's medications, contacts, and recent health notes — ready to hand to a new caregiver or bring to the ER.
Role-based access
Invite family, friends, and caregivers with the right level of access — admins, contributors, or viewers. Everyone sees exactly what they need to.

Smart assistance

A little help when you need it

Vardfolk quietly works in the background to make coordination easier — so your family can focus on what matters.

Before every appointment
Get a personalized summary of recent health updates, current medications, and suggested questions — prepared automatically so whoever's going feels ready and informed.
Spot the actionable moments
When a journal entry mentions something that needs follow-up — a call to make, a prescription to refill — Vardfolk offers to turn it into a task. One tap, done.
Kinder communication
Family stress is real. When a message sounds harsher than intended, a gentle suggestion helps you say what you mean in a way that brings people together instead of pulling them apart.
Contacts, remembered for you
As your circle logs appointments and uploads documents, Vardfolk quietly notices new doctors, pharmacies, and care providers — and offers to add them to your contacts.
