1. What Vardfolk is — and what it is not

Vardfolk is a care coordination platform that helps families and care teams organize and communicate around the care of an aging or ill loved one — tasks, appointments, medications, documents, messages, and updates, shared within a “care circle.”

Vardfolk is a coordination and organization tool only. It is not a medical provider, does not provide medical, clinical, pharmacological, or professional advice, and is not a substitute for the judgment of qualified healthcare professionals. Medications, allergies, appointments, and other health-related information are stored for coordination purposes only. Vardfolk does not provide drug-interaction checking, dosing guidance, or clinical decision support. Always rely on your healthcare providers and original documents for medical decisions.

Medication reminders are a convenience. They depend on your phone’s settings and connection, stop when you sign out, and can be late or missed; they are never a substitute for the schedule your doctor or pharmacist gave you. “Due now,” “overdue” and “missed” mean only that no dose was logged against the schedule your circle entered.

Some Vardfolk features use artificial intelligence (described in our Privacy Policy). AI-assisted output can be inaccurate or incomplete and must not be relied upon for medical decisions. Always verify important information — especially dates, dosages, and medical details — against your original documents.

Vardfolk is made for families and the people who help them. It is not for use by health-care providers, home-care agencies, health plans, or anyone acting on their behalf to manage patient information, and it is not designed to comply with HIPAA, GLBA or other industry-specific laws. If your use would be subject to such laws, you may not use the Services for that purpose.
