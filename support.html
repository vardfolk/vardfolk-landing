Support

We’re here to help

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Vardfolk is a small, independent app from California that helps families and friends share the care of someone they love. If something isn’t working, or you just have a question, write to us. A real person reads every message.

  • Questions, problems, feedbackhello@vardfolk.com
  • Privacy and your dataprivacy@vardfolk.com

Contact us

Email hello@vardfolk.com and we’ll reply within two business days. It helps to tell us which device you’re using (iPhone, iPad, Android or the web) and what you were trying to do — a screenshot is welcome but never needed.

For anything about your personal information — what we keep, getting a copy of it, or having it deleted — write to privacy@vardfolk.com.

You can also reach us from inside the app: Help & Guide → Contact us.

Common questions

Most how-to questions are answered right in the app, in Help & Guide. One you can’t look up there:

Didn’t get your sign-in code or invitation? Check your spam or junk folder, and wait a minute before asking for a new code — then use the newest email. Still nothing? Write to hello@vardfolk.com.

Reporting something in your circle

Care circles are private and invite-only, and most of what’s shared in them is kind. If someone posts something that isn’t, you can tell us straight from the app:

  • A message: press and hold it (or use its ⋯ menu on a computer) and choose Report.
  • An Update or a comment: open its ⋯ menu and choose Report.
  • A photo or a file: in the Vault, open its ⋯ menu and choose Report. While you’re looking at a photo, tap the flag in its top-left corner.

We look at every report within 24 hours. If something breaks our Terms, we remove it, and we may remove the person from the circle or close their account. We won’t tell the person you report who reported them, unless the law requires us to.

You can report anything someone else wrote, whatever your role in the circle. If you’d rather explain in your own words, email hello@vardfolk.com.

Hiding a person

Sometimes you need a little distance from someone, even while you’re caring for the same person. You can hide them, and you decide for how long.

  • To hide someone: in Manage circle → Members, tap them and choose Hide. You can also choose it from the ⋯ menu on a message, Update or comment they wrote (or press and hold a message). It applies in every circle you share.
  • What changes for you: their messages, Updates and comments fold into a quiet line — “Hidden message from …” — with nothing of what they wrote in it, and you’re no longer notified about them. Tap Show if you ever want to see one.
  • What stays: the care itself. Medicines and doses they log, tasks and appointments they add or complete, and documents in the Vault still show, so nobody misses a skipped dose or a moved appointment.
  • They aren’t told, and nothing changes for them or anyone else in the circle.
  • To undo it: Manage circle → Members, tap them, then Unhide.

If someone in your circle is being abusive, please report it too, so we can step in. A circle’s admin can also remove a member, and anyone can leave a circle at any time — if you’re its only admin, the app asks you to make someone else an admin first.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account whenever you like, right in the app: Settings → Account → Danger Zone → Delete account. If you haven’t joined or created a circle yet, the same Danger Zone sits under Sign out on the “Create your care circle” screen.

What happens to your circles

  • A circle where you’re the only member is deleted with everything in it — tasks, appointments, medications, Updates, messages, contacts and stored files.
  • A circle with other members carries on for them. You’re removed, and what you wrote stays for the circle without your name on it.
  • If you’re the only admin of a circle with other members, the app asks you to make someone else an admin first (or to remove the other members), so the people still caring together don’t lose their circle.

If you subscribe to Plus

A subscription bought on the web is cancelled when your account is deleted. A subscription bought through the App Store or Google Play is billed by Apple or Google, so deleting your account doesn’t stop it — please cancel it first in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions. The app links you there from Settings → Billing → Manage subscription.

Without the app

Email privacy@vardfolk.com from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We’ll confirm the request is yours, delete the account and its data as described above, and write back when it’s done — within 7 days. More detail is in section 7 of our Privacy Policy.

Privacy and the assistant

Vardfolk’s assistant can read documents, summarize what’s been happening and suggest next steps. It’s powered by Anthropic. Anthropic doesn’t use what it reads to train its models.

The first time you use one of the assistant’s features, or add a document, we ask whether the assistant may read what you add, and you choose Allow or Not now. If you choose Not now, the Updates, messages, comments and documents you add are never sent to Anthropic. A document you upload is still read by our own server so you can find it in search, and nothing else is drawn from it. Shared care details your circle keeps together, like medications and appointments, are used by the assistant when your circle’s admin has it on.

You can change your mind anytime in Settings → Account → Assistant. A circle’s admin can turn the assistant off for the whole circle in Manage circle.
