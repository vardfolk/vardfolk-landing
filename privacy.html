Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Vardfolk (“Vardfolk,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, shares, and protects your information when you use our website at vardfolk.com, our applications at app.vardfolk.com and on iOS and Android, and related services (together, the “Services”).

Vardfolk is a care coordination platform. Families and care teams use it to organize and communicate around the care of an aging or ill loved one — tracking tasks, appointments, medications, documents, messages, and updates in a shared “care circle.” Vardfolk is a coordination tool. It is not a medical provider, is not a substitute for professional medical advice, and is not intended for clinical decision-making.

Vardfolk is operated by Vardfolk, a sole proprietorship, located in California, United States. If you have questions about this policy or your information, contact us at privacy@vardfolk.com.

Summary of key points

  • What we collect. Account and profile information; the care information you and your circle members enter (care-recipient details, medications, allergies, appointments, tasks, journal entries, messages, contacts, wellbeing check-ins); documents you upload (which may include health-related files); billing information; and technical/device information for notifications and security.
  • Sensitive information. Because Vardfolk is used to coordinate care, the information you provide may include health-related details about a care recipient. You provide this voluntarily to coordinate care.
  • How we use AI. Vardfolk uses artificial intelligence to power features such as document scanning, summaries, and suggestions. To do this, relevant content — which may include health information — is sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, to generate the result. Anthropic does not use this content to train its AI models.
  • Who we share with. We share information with the service providers that operate Vardfolk. Within the app, your care-circle information is visible to the members of that circle according to their role. We do not sell your personal information.
  • Your choices. You can review, update, export, or delete your information, and delete your account, at any time.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide directly

  • Account and profile information — your name, email address, and a password, or authentication through Apple or Google sign-in. Optionally, a profile photo.
  • Care-circle and care-recipient information — the name and details of the person being cared for, and the members you invite to the circle.
  • Health-related care information — medications, dosages and schedules, allergies, appointments, and the contents of documents you upload (such as discharge summaries, after-visit summaries, insurance cards, and other health-related files).
  • Coordination content — tasks, journal entries and care updates, messages you send within a circle, important contacts, and wellbeing check-ins.
  • Billing information — when you subscribe to a paid plan, your payment details are collected and processed by Stripe. Vardfolk does not store your full card number.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and technical information — including push-notification tokens (for Apple and web push), and basic information needed to operate and secure the Services.
  • Usage and diagnostic information — when you use the app, we and our analytics and crash-reporting providers automatically collect limited information about how the Services are used (such as which features are opened and general device and session information) and diagnostic information (such as crash reports and performance data), to help us understand usage and fix technical problems. This information is associated with a randomly generated identifier rather than your name or email address. We configure these providers so that location derived from your IP address is discarded and is not stored, and so that the care content you store in Vardfolk — documents, messages, medications, and journal entries — is excluded from what they collect. See Section 4 for the providers we use for this purpose.

Sensitive information. The care information you enter may be considered sensitive in some jurisdictions because it relates to health. You provide this information voluntarily in order to coordinate care. Vardfolk is not a HIPAA-covered entity, and the Services are not designed to comply with HIPAA, and should not be used in any way that would require HIPAA compliance.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Create and maintain your account and authenticate you.
  • Provide the Services — store and display your care circle’s tasks, appointments, medications, documents, messages, and updates to the members of that circle.
  • Provide AI-assisted features (see Section 3).
  • Send notifications and service-related communications.
  • Process payments and manage subscriptions.
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the Services and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • Comply with legal obligations.
  • Understand how the Services are used, and detect and fix technical problems, so we can keep the app reliable and improve it.

3. How we use artificial intelligence

Vardfolk uses artificial intelligence to make care coordination easier. AI-assisted features include scanning and reading uploaded documents to extract medications, appointments, and tasks; generating summaries and briefings; suggesting tasks from journal entries; suggesting document tags and contacts; and helping draft notes and messages.

To provide these features, relevant content is sent to our AI provider, Anthropic (the maker of Claude). Depending on the feature, this content may include text extracted from documents you upload (which may contain health information), journal entries, messages, appointment and medication details, and other care information.

Anthropic processes this content to generate the feature’s output and, under its commercial API terms, does not use it to train its AI models.

How we handle the results:

  • Document scanning and extraction. The text read from your uploaded document and the structured information extracted from it (such as medications, appointments, and tasks) are saved in your care circle’s records, so you can review and use them. They are deleted when you delete the document or the care circle.
  • Other AI features (summaries, briefings, suggestions, drafting help) process your information to generate a response. Most of these are shown to you in the moment and are not stored — a briefing or a drafted note is kept only if you choose to keep it, for example by accepting a suggested task.
  • Some AI-generated content is saved as part of how the feature works, so that it is there when you come back to it. Suggested contacts found in your circle’s notes are saved so they are waiting for you to review, accept, or dismiss; a document you scan may be given a name and a tag drawn from its own contents; and a preparation brief generated for an appointment is kept on that appointment, so you can read it again before the visit. Anything saved this way lives in your care circle’s records alongside the rest of your information, and is deleted when you delete that item or the care circle.
  • We do not keep separate logs of the content sent to, or received from, the AI provider.

AI-generated output can contain mistakes. Vardfolk does not provide medical advice, and AI-assisted features are aids to coordination, not clinical guidance. Always verify important details — especially dates, dosages, and medical information — against your original documents and your healthcare providers.

This sharing is how Vardfolk works — it lets your family and care team stay coordinated. Your care-circle information is not public and is not shared outside your circle except as described below.

5. Where your information is stored

Vardfolk is operated in the United States, and your information is stored and processed in the United States. If you access the Services from outside the United States, you understand that your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States.

6. How long we keep your information

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Services. When information is deleted (see Section 7), it is removed from our active systems. Residual copies may persist temporarily in routine backups before being overwritten, and we may retain limited information where required to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements.

7. Deleting your information and your account

You can delete your account at any time from the app’s account settings, or by contacting us at privacy@vardfolk.com. Here is what happens when you delete your account:

  • If you are the only member of a care circle, that circle and all of its data are permanently deleted — including tasks, appointments, medications, journal entries, messages, contacts, and the uploaded files stored for that circle.
  • If a care circle has other members, the circle and its shared records remain so the other members can continue using them. Your membership is removed, and your personal connection to content you created is removed (your authorship is cleared). Comments you posted on tasks, appointments, and journal entries, your wellbeing check-ins, your notifications, and your notification settings are deleted.
  • If you are the only administrator of a circle that still has other members, you will be asked to make another member an administrator, or to remove the other members, before you can delete your account — so the circle is not left without an owner.
  • Your profile, your authentication account, and your device/push tokens are deleted.

You may also delete individual documents, messages, and other items at any time, and delete an entire care circle you administer.

8. How we protect your information

We use technical and organizational measures designed to protect your information, including encryption in transit, access controls, and authentication. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping your account credentials confidential.

9. Children’s privacy

The Services are intended for adults (18 and older) coordinating care. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. A care recipient’s information is entered by the adult members of a care circle. If you believe a child under 18 has provided us with personal information, contact us at privacy@vardfolk.com and we will delete it.
